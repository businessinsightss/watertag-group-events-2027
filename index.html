Can I add WaterTag to my existing event business?

Yes. WaterTag markets equipment to both new and existing operators. Discuss your customer base and event format with the sales team to choose a setup.

Is the equipment mobile?

WaterTag describes its equipment as mobile, with collapsible barrels for transport. Confirm venue requirements, water access, and the selected package before planning an event.

How much revenue can my events generate?

Revenue depends on actual bookings, ticket prices, and session volume. The calculator on this page illustrates gross revenue only; it does not predict demand or profit.

How do I get a quote for 2027?

Email the sales team with your location, intended event formats, expected group sizes, and target launch date. Ask for current pricing and delivery details.
